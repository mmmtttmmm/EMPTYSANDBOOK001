2 · Decision 1: CIS reverse charge where the invoice has no VAT wording

The policy question still stands. For CIS-verified, labour-only invoices with no VAT or reverse-charge wording, what should the default be: (a) domestic reverse charge, (b) outside scope because the subcontractor isn't VAT-registered, or (c) case by case? The input-VAT effect is nil either way. The choice decides how the VAT return boxes are filled and keeps the treatment consistent with CIS.

Use live examples, not the brief's table. The August and September email invoices from Carlos Faria (£180/day) and Nelson Ferreira (£200/day) are exactly this case: labour less 20% CIS, with no VAT wording.
